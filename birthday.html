You’ve Always Been More Than Just A Sister

Dearest Didi, from our very earliest childhood memories to the present day, you have been my greatest champion, my sweetest comfort, and the warmest light in my life. Whenever life felt daunting, knowing that my older sister stood beside me gave me an unshakeable sense of security.

You shared your treats, forgave my mischief, guided my steps when I faltered, and taught me with gentle patience what it truly means to be kind, courageous, and dignified. Your heart is genuinely made of gold, and your laughter can heal any dull day.

On this 20th of October, as you celebrate your birthday, I want to remind you that your dreams are destined to come true. May you continue to soar to incredible heights, wear that radiant smile forever, and know that your little brother will always admire, respect, and love you beyond measure.

With infinite love & blessings, Your Younger Brother ❤️
